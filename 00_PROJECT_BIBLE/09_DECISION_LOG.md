## Decision 003: Top Customer Value Gate

**Date:** 8 August 2026

**Decision:**
Before any NEXUS feature, task, AI employee work item, workflow, automation, integration, or initiative proceeds, NEXUS must answer:

`Kya ye paying customer lane ya existing customer ko result dene me help karta hai?`

YES requires evidence that the work helps acquire a paying customer or helps an existing customer obtain a meaningful result.

NO, UNKNOWN, unsupported, or guessed value must be treated as NO and the work must be rejected or deprioritized.

**Safety Boundary:**
Passing the Customer Value Gate never bypasses owner control, safety, tenant isolation, auditability, security, privacy, legal/compliance, financial, production, or external-action controls.

**Status:** Founder Approved and Permanent
