\---



\## 5. NAMING OBJECTIVES



The preferred legal company name should be:



1\. Distinctive.

2\. Professional.

3\. Globally credible.

4\. Easy to pronounce.

5\. Easy to remember.

6\. Suitable for an AI/technology company.

7\. Broad enough for future products and markets.

8\. Capable of supporting NEXUS as a product brand.

9\. Free from misleading or inappropriate implications.

10\. Practical for long-term corporate use.



\---



\## 6. AVOID



Avoid names that are:



1\. Confusingly similar to existing companies or major brands.

2\. Unnecessarily long or difficult to spell.

3\. Too narrowly tied to one industry if that restricts future expansion.

4\. Dependent on the old Celeris/proprietorship identity.

5\. Likely to create trademark conflicts.

6\. Likely to create regulatory or company-name approval problems.

7\. Selected solely because a domain appears available.

8\. Presented as approved before official verification.



\---



\## 7. REQUIRED VERIFICATION BEFORE FINAL APPROVAL



Before the Founder approves the final legal company name, the candidate should undergo appropriate checks for:



\- Indian company-name availability and applicable naming rules.

\- Existing registered or applied-for trademarks where relevant.

\- Materially similar business/technology brands.

\- Suitable domain options.

\- Relevant social/public identity conflicts.

\- Pronunciation and spelling risk.

\- International meaning or obvious negative interpretation where relevant.

\- Long-term suitability for NEXUS and future products.



\---



\## 8. COST-CONTROL RULE



No domain, trademark service, naming service, incorporation package, or other naming-related paid service will be purchased merely to reserve a proposed idea without Founder approval.



Free and official verification routes should be used first wherever practical.



Any paid recommendation must consider:



\- government/statutory fees;

\- professional fees;

\- taxes;

\- renewal costs;

\- hidden charges;

\- lock-in;

\- support quality;

\- compliance quality;

\- total cost rather than headline price alone.



\---



\## 9. DECISION AUTHORITY



AI systems, vendors, consultants, incorporation providers, domain registrars, and other third parties may research or recommend names.



They cannot independently approve the final NEXUS legal parent-company name.



Final approval remains with the Founder.
