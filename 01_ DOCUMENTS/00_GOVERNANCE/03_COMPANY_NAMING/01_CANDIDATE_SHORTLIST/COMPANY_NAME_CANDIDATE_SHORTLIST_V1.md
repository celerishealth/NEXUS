\## 2. NAMING OBJECTIVE



The final legal parent-company name should support NEXUS as a globally credible AI and technology company.

\- distinctive;

\- professional;

\- globally credible;

\- easy to pronounce;

\- easy to remember;

\- suitable for AI/software/technology;

\- broad enough for future products;

\- suitable for long-term corporate use;

\- capable of supporting NEXUS as a product brand.
