\## 1. PURPOSE



This register is the master index for controlled NEXUS company, legal, governance, incorporation, compliance, ownership, financial-control, credential-control, and launch-readiness documents.



It records document existence and status.



A document appearing in this register does not by itself mean that the underlying legal action, filing, payment, registration, approval, or compliance requirement has been completed.



\---



\## 2. DOCUMENT CONTROL PRINCIPLES



1\. Founder-approved and locked decisions must not be silently overwritten.

2\. Material changes require controlled revision.

3\. Historical evidence must be preserved where required.

4\. Drafts must not be represented as executed legal documents.

5\. Proposed items must not be represented as approved.

6\. Unverified information must remain marked PENDING or TO VERIFY.

7\. Sensitive credentials must not be stored in this register.

8\. No document creates payment, filing, signing, banking, production, or public-launch authority unless that authority is explicitly granted.

9\. Company/document work must remain distinguishable from NEXUS software-development evidence.

10\. Public launch remains separately gated.

\## 7. PUBLIC-LAUNCH CONTROL



Build completion and company/legal readiness are separate gates.



Reaching Build Day 811 does not automatically authorize public launch.



Public launch remains blocked until:



1\. required company/legal/document-control work is completed and verified;

2\. applicable operational launch blockers are resolved;

3\. final readiness is verified;

4\. Founder explicitly approves public launch.



Countdown reaching zero must never be interpreted as automatic launch authorization.

\## 8. DOCUMENT STATUS DEFINITIONS



DRAFT:

Document exists but is not an approved controlled baseline.



ACTIVE WORKING:

Document is actively used but contains unresolved items.



ACTIVE CONTROLLED:

Document is part of the controlled company/document system.



PENDING:

Required decision, evidence, or action is incomplete.



TO VERIFY:

Claim or evidence requires verification.



SUPERSEDED:

A newer controlled version replaces the document while history remains preserved.



BLOCKED:

The related action cannot proceed until required conditions are satisfied.



COMPLETED:

The applicable requirement has been completed and verified.



\---



\## 9. CHANGE CONTROL



When a new controlled document is created:



1\. assign a unique MDR identifier;

2\. record its exact filename and path;

3\. assign category and status;

4\. state its purpose;

5\. preserve prior versions when material history matters;

6\. update this Master Document Register.



No important company document should be considered controlled merely because it exists somewhere on disk.



\---



\## 10. OWNER AUTHORITY



Founder/Owner retains final authority over reserved company matters.



AI systems may assist with research, drafting, organization, checking, and evidence preparation within authorized boundaries.



AI systems do not independently obtain authority to:



\- incorporate the company;

\- sign legal documents;

\- make material payments;

\- alter ownership;

\- open/control bank accounts;

\- create unrestricted permanent credentials;

\- commit the company to material legal obligations;

\- authorize public launch.
