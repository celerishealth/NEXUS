## 7. FOUNDER CONTROL PRINCIPLES

The intended structure should preserve:

1. Founder strategic control.
2. Founder approval over reserved matters.
3. Protection against unintended dilution.
4. Protection against unauthorized ownership changes.
5. Clear voting/control rights.
6. Controlled transfer of shares.
7. Clear succession/continuity rules where appropriate.
8. No external ownership without explicit Founder approval.
